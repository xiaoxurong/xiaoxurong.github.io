Efficient adaptationUnder review · ICLR 2027

RIFT

Sparse Representation Fine-Tuning with Random Support

How little needs to change to adapt a language model? RIFT trains sparse edits to hidden representations while keeping the pretrained model weights frozen.

I study random versus gradient-based support selection and the tradeoff between task performance and trainable parameter budget, with evaluations on the Qwen2.5 family and RoBERTa.

  • Representation fine-tuning
  • Language models
  • Random support

Benchmarks include commonsense reasoning, GLUE, and GSM8K.

Data constraintsPublic-data benchmark

Separating signal
from shortcuts

Foundation models for clinical time series

Using public fetal heart-rate recordings, I compare models trained from scratch with frozen pretrained encoders. The central question is whether predictions reflect the heart-rate trace or shortcuts such as signal loss and recording length.

The benchmark uses subject-level cross-validation, shortcut baselines, and confirmation on new folds. It connects my neonatal encephalopathy research to a reproducible public-data study of fetal acidemia.

  • Time-series transfer
  • Shortcut analysis
  • Clinical ML

View benchmark results and evaluation context +

The public CTU–UHB benchmark contains 541 usable recordings. The figure reports the initial repeated cross-validation results; the rank-averaged combination is exploratory. In a subsequent confirmation on new folds at pH < 7.05, the combination reached AUROC 0.749 versus 0.722 for the shortcut baseline. The paired improvement was +0.027 (95% interval −0.016 to +0.071), so the gain remains uncertain. External validation is pending.

Figures and values: NE-code, public results and confirmation tables. This public acidemia benchmark uses a different outcome and cohort from the PAS 2025 neonatal encephalopathy poster.

Generalized nonnegative matrix factorization for data that lie in a union of subspaces. This work combines matrix factorization and subspace clustering to learn localized, interpretable representations.
