Projects

RIFT: fine-tuning language models through sparse representation edits

Paper under review

RIFT adapts a language model by making sparse edits to its hidden-state representations instead of updating its weights, training only 1,024 scalars per layer. I implemented it alongside LoRA and LoReFT baselines in PyTorch on the Qwen2.5 model family and evaluated on commonsense reasoning, GLUE, and GSM8K, running the experiments on an HTCondor GPU cluster.

PyTorch · LLM fine-tuning · PEFT · HTCondor

Predicting neonatal encephalopathy from cardiotocography

Presented at the Pediatric Academic Societies Meeting, 2025

A CNN–LSTM model for cardiotocography (fetal heart rate and uterine contraction) signals, pretrained on a public dataset and fine-tuned with LoRA on a small clinical dataset. Transfer raised AUC from 0.50 to 0.64 on the clinical test set.

PyTorch · time series · transfer learning · LoRA

Benchmarking multimodal foundation models for medical imaging

A benchmark of five biomedical image–text models (PubMedCLIP, ConVIRT, MedCLIP, BioViL, BiomedCLIP) on chest X-ray classification, including zero-shot and few-shot settings and the effect of batch size and learning-rate schedule. The longer-term goal is adapting these models to ovarian cancer imaging, where labeled data is scarce.

PyTorch · vision–language models · few-shot learning

Code

Generalized nonnegative matrix factorization

Standard NMF learns one global basis and performs poorly when data lies in a union of subspaces. This framework combines NMF with subspace clustering to learn localized, interpretable representations, evaluated on synthetic data and on image, text, and gene-expression datasets.

Python · clustering · matrix factorization
