## Xiaoxu Rong

Ph.D. student in Biomedical Data Science and concurrent M.S. student in Computer Science at UW–Madison. I develop and evaluate machine learning methods that make the most of limited data and computation.

**Open to Summer 2027 Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist internships.**

### Research directions

- **Learning from limited and imperfect data:** transfer learning, multimodal models, and structured representations for noisy, scarce, or heterogeneous data, with biomedical problems as a demanding testbed.
- **Efficient adaptation of foundation models:** parameter-efficient fine-tuning and sparse interventions on learned representations.

### Projects

- **RIFT: Sparse Representation Fine-Tuning with Random Support**: sparse hidden-representation interventions for adapting language models.
- **[NE-code](https://github.com/xiaoxurong/NE-code)**: predicting fetal acidemia from labor heart-rate recordings (CTU-UHB), comparing a CNN–LSTM with frozen pretrained time-series encoders against shortcut baselines.
- **[Benchmarking-Multimodal-CXR](https://github.com/xiaoxurong/Benchmarking-Multimodal-CXR)**: zero-shot and few-shot benchmark of five biomedical image–text foundation models on chest X-rays.
- **[Generalized-NMF](https://github.com/xiaoxurong/Generalized-NMF)**: nonnegative matrix factorization for data in a union of subspaces.
